How to use RevenueForge

  1. Set up your robot in Settings — skills, target, and alert channels.
  2. Choose a plan in Pricing — it defines how many jobs/actions per month and unlocks WhatsApp/Telegram alerts.
  3. Check “My Jobs” — your robot delivers scored matches with ready proposal drafts.
  4. Get alerts on WhatsApp/Telegram the moment you win or sell (Growth and above).
  5. Run the free audit anytime to preview new opportunities.
